Cookie policy

We set exactly one cookie, and it only keeps you signed in.

Last updated 6 October 2026 · Version 1.3

The cookie we set

When you sign in, we store a session token in your browser’s local storage. It identifies you to our server so you do not have to log in again. It expires after seven days, and it disappears the moment you sign out.

That is the only thing we store in your browser. There are no advertising cookies, no cross-site trackers, and no third-party analytics scripts on this site.

What we do not use

  • No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or equivalent.
  • No advertising exchanges or retargeting tags.
  • No social media embeds that load their own cookies.

Why so few

A news site that asks people to trust it should not be quietly shipping their browsing behaviour to third parties. We count page views on our own server instead, and we only ever see them as totals.

Clearing it

Sign out from the staff desk, or clear local storage for this site in your browser settings, and the token is gone. Nothing else is stored, so there is nothing else to clear.

Read the full privacy policy for how we handle the data on our side, or contact us with any question.
