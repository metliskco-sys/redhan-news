About Redhan

An independent newsroom for a nation that is almost all sea

Redhan reports from the Maldives in English — from the newsrooms of Malé out to the outermost atolls. We publish what is verified, name our sources where we can, and say plainly when we do not know.

Redhan Media Pvt. Ltd. was founded in 2019 by a group of Maldivian journalists who wanted the country's story told by people who live it, in the language the outside world reads.

We are a small newsroom: writers, editors, a photo desk and a data reporter. We do not have a foreign bureau network. What we do have is a boat schedule, a contacts book built over a decade, and the patience to wait for the tide.

What we cover

  • Politics — the People's Majlis, cabinet and local council decisions, and what they mean for island communities.
  • Business — tourism receipts, fisheries, foreign investment and the digital economy.
  • Climate & environment — reef health, sea-level rise, waste and the growing adaptation bill.
  • Culture — language, music, craft and the everyday life of the atolls.

How we work

Every story is edited by a second person before it publishes. Numbers are traced back to a named source — a ministry report, a published dataset, a company filing — and we link to it. When a claim cannot be confirmed, we say so in the copy rather than hedging elsewhere.

We do not accept payment for coverage. Advertising is sold separately, is clearly labelled, and never shapes what we report. Sponsored content is marked and sits outside editorial control.

If the story is worth telling, it is worth telling properly — sourced, verified, and in its own voice.

Corrections

We get things wrong and we fix them in the open. Corrections appear at the foot of the article with the date and what changed. If you spot an error, write to our editors and we will respond within one working day.
