Privacy policy

We collect as little as we can, we never sell it, and we will delete it when you ask.

Last updated 6 October 2026 · Version 2.1

Redhan Media Pvt. Ltd. (“Redhan”, “we”) runs this website. This policy explains what personal data we handle when you read, comment on, or subscribe to our journalism.

1. What we collect

  • Account data — your name, email address and role, if you register. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes; we cannot read them.
  • Comments — the name, email and text you submit. Email addresses are never shown publicly.
  • Server logs — IP address, user agent and the page requested. Retained for 30 days.
  • Analytics — aggregate counts of pages read. We do not build advertising profiles.

2. Why we hold it

To run your account, to display your comments, to keep the site secure, and to understand roughly how many people read us. We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

3. What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • We do not share reader data with advertisers — only aggregate figures.
  • We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

4. Cookies

A single cookie stores your sign-in token. We set no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. See our cookie policy.

5. How long we keep it

Accounts and comment data are kept while your account exists, or while a comment remains published. Server logs are deleted after 30 days.

6. Your rights

You may ask us for a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it. Write to privacy@redhan.mv and we will act within 30 days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Authority of the Maldives.

7. Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS. Staff access is role-based and logged. No system is perfect; if a breach affected your data we would notify you and the authority within 72 hours.

Questions about this policy go to privacy@redhan.mv. For anything urgent, call +960 300 1234 during office hours in Malé.
