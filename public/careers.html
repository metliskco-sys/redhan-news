Work at Redhan

We hire slowly, pay fairly and train people from the islands. If you write well and want to be read by everyone, we want to hear from you.

Redhan is a six-person newsroom with a large appetite. We value clear writing over credentials, and we would rather train someone who asks good questions than hire a CV.

How we hire

  • Send three paragraphs about a story you would report for us — not a cover letter.
  • If it is a fit, a 45-minute call with the editor-in-chief.
  • One paid test piece. Everyone who submits one gets a reply.

What we offer

Salaries benchmarked to the Maldivian market, 26 days of leave, a training budget, and travel to the atolls when the story requires it. Remote work is possible for editing roles; reporting roles are based in Malé.

We particularly encourage applicants from outside Malé. If travel to an interview is a barrier, tell us and we will arrange it.
